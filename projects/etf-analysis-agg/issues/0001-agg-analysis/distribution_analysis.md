AGG Distribution Analysis

**Payment frequency and recent distribution:** AGG pays distributions monthly, which distinguishes it from equity ETFs referenced in the data (QQQ and SCHD, both quarterly). The most recent reported distribution was $0.330 per share, paid 2026-05-06. The data lists a next ex-dividend date of 2026-08-03 with an estimated forward distribution of $0.3307 per share — roughly in line with the prior payment, suggesting a relatively stable monthly payout at this snapshot, though only two data points (one actual, one estimated) are provided, which is not enough to characterize a trend.

**Yield figures — explicit inconsistency:** The data reports three different yield figures from different sources, and they should not be treated as interchangeable or averaged:
- 3.85%, as of 2026-08-04
- 4.04%, described as an annualized figure based on an expected annual distribution of $3.94/share
- 4.07%, described as based on the most recent distribution

These figures differ by roughly 20 basis points depending on methodology (point-in-time yield vs. annualized-from-projected-total vs. annualized-from-latest-payment) and reporting date, and the underlying source pages (financecharts.com, macrotrends.net, stockinvest.us, marketchameleon.com) were not cross-verified against AGG's official iShares fact sheet in this data collection. The data explicitly flags this as an unresolved discrepancy rather than something reconcilable from what's provided. No single "correct" yield figure can be identified from this data — a reader should treat AGG's yield as approximately in the high-3%-to-just-above-4% range, with the exact figure sensitive to methodology and date.

**Relative positioning:** Within the three funds referenced in the data, AGG's yield (~4%, using any of the three figures) is the highest, above SCHD (~3%) and substantially above QQQ (0.42%). This is consistent with AGG being a bond fund rather than an equity fund, though the data does not provide a duration, credit-quality, or rate-environment explanation for this differential — it simply reports the comparison.

**Data limitations (as documented in the source material):**
- The yield discrepancy (3.85%/4.04%/4.07%) is unresolved and drawn from different sources/dates without cross-verification against the official iShares fact sheet or Bloomberg index methodology.
- No tracking-error figure was found for AGG.
- The YTD return figure (-0.47%) mentioned in the broader dataset has no specified as-of date for its calculation.
- A macro-commentary similarity noted between sources was not cross-verified and may reflect different dates/origins.
- All data reflects a search snapshot as of 2026-08-13, not live pricing; the underlying fact sheet reference point is 2026-06-30, introducing additional lag.

Given these gaps — particularly the three-way yield inconsistency and the missing tracking-error data — this dataset supports only a general characterization of AGG's income profile (monthly payer, mid-single-digit yield range) and does not support a precise, single-figure yield conclusion.
