The data translates this into a practical sensitivity estimate: a 1 percentage point move in interest rates could move the fund's price by approximately 5.85% in the opposite direction (a duration-based estimate). This is a meaningful level of rate sensitivity — a fund with ~5.8 years of effective duration will see materially larger price swings from rate changes than a short-duration bond fund, and this interest-rate risk is likely the dominant risk factor for a fund of this type, though the data does not provide a convexity measure or scenario analysis (e.g., non-parallel yield curve shifts) that would refine this simple linear duration estimate.

**Summary**

Taken together, the data describes a fund with a negative YTD return set against a modestly positive one-year total return, an SEC yield and YTD figure that are internally plausible but incompletely dated, moderate-to-material interest rate sensitivity (effective duration of 5.80 years, translating to an estimated ~5.85% price move per 1-point rate change), volatility roughly half that of the equity-oriented comparison fund in this dataset, and low but non-trivial equity market correlation (beta 0.23).
