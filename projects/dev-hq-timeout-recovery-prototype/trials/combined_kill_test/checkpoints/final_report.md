## Bull Case

## Bear Case

## Synthesis
Both cases largely cite the *same* figures — the divide is interpretation, not data. Genuine unresolved factual conflicts include: market cap ($258.56B vs. $385B), DCF fair values/baselines ($113.62/$77.76 vs. $179.01/$102), and unreconciled CHF/USD figures throughout. The single highest-leverage open question is what drives the GAAP-vs-adjusted earnings gap, since it also determines whether the 86.9% payout ratio is dangerously understated. Other consequential gaps: China-specific growth, the CEO investigation's substance, and sourcing for the competitive-threat claim. Given these unresolved inconsistencies, the data as provided does not support a confident, unified conclusion in either direction.
