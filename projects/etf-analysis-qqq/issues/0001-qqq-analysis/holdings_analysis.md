## QQQ Holdings Concentration Analysis

The data provided shows QQQ (Invesco QQQ Trust, tracking the Nasdaq-100) carries meaningful single-name and top-heavy concentration, though the exact magnitude varies depending on which source is used.

**Single-name dominance:** NVIDIA appears as the largest holding across all three sources, ranging from 8.46% (Source A) to ~8.7% (Source B) to 8.9% (Source C). Apple is consistently the second-largest position, at 7.32% (A), ~7.1% (B), and 7.2% (C) — these three figures are reasonably tight and mutually consistent. A fund's largest single holding sitting at roughly 8-9% of assets means that stock's price movements alone can have an outsized effect on the fund's overall return, independent of how the other 100+ constituents perform.

**Top-5 concentration:** Source A lists a top-5 (NVIDIA, Apple, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon.com) summing to 8.46 + 7.32 + 6.44 + 5.93 + 4.68 = 32.83%. Source B gives a similar but not identical top-5 (NVIDIA, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon — notably omitting Alphabet from the list shown) and states the top-5 sum as "약 30%" (~30%), roughly a percentage point or two below what Source A's own figures would imply if extrapolated with a fifth name. This is a modest but real inconsistency: Source B's stated aggregate doesn't clearly reconcile with the four names it lists, since a fifth holding isn't specified.

**Top-10 concentration:** Source C states the top-10 combined weight at 47.3%. This is a materially different lens than the top-5 figures from A and B, and the data provided doesn't include a full top-10 list from any source, so it can't be cross-checked holding-by-holding against A or B. Taking Source A's top-5 (~32.8%) and Source C's top-10 (47.3%) together at face value would imply holdings ranked 6-10 contribute roughly 14.5 percentage points, but this is an inference bridging two different sources collected at different, unspecified times — not a figure stated directly in the data.

**Inconsistencies to flag explicitly:**
- Exact weights for the same holding differ across sources (e.g., NVIDIA 8.46% vs ~8.7% vs 8.9%; Microsoft 5.93% vs ~5.3% vs 5.0%), which the source material attributes to differing collection timestamps rather than any correction or restatement.
- Source B's top-5 list and its stated ~30% aggregate are not fully reconcilable with only four names disclosed.
- No single source in this dataset provides a complete, consistent top-10 list with individual weights — Source C gives the aggregate (47.3%) plus only three individual names, not a full breakdown.
- The data itself notes that Nasdaq-100 rebalancing and ongoing price movements mean rankings and weights "may differ somewhat" from precise mid-August 2026 values, so none of these figures should be read as a single authoritative snapshot.

**Limitations:** This analysis relies solely on the web-search snapshot data given above; it has not been cross-verified against Invesco's official fact sheet or SEC filings. Given the source-to-source variance in reported weights and the incomplete top-10 breakdown, the data is sufficient to establish that concentration is meaningful (single largest holding ~8-9%, top-5 roughly 30-33%, top-10 near 47%) but is not sufficient to pin down precise, reconciled figures as of a specific date.
