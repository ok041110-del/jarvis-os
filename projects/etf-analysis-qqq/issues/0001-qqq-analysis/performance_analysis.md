## Performance and Volatility Analysis: Invesco QQQ Trust, Series 1 (QQQ)

**Return performance across periods**

The data provided shows QQQ delivering strong positive returns across all measured periods, though the figures come from different sources and reference dates, which limits direct comparability. On a dividend-reinvested basis measured as of August 4, 2026, the fund's year-to-date return was +18.11%. A separate NAV-based YTD figure as of June 30, 2026 shows +20.19% — a different figure from a different date and a different return basis (NAV vs. dividend-reinvested total return), so the two YTD numbers should not be treated as restatements of the same underlying quantity. The one-year dividend-reinvested return was +28.94%.

Longer-horizon returns of 26.55% (3-year), 16.45% (5-year), and 22.07% (10-year) were also reported. The data explicitly notes that whether these are annualized figures is not specified in the source material. This is a material gap: if these are cumulative rather than annualized returns, their interpretation — and any comparison across the three periods — would differ substantially. As given, no conclusion can be drawn about the fund's compounded annual growth rate over these horizons.

For the second quarter of 2026 specifically, QQQ returned +27.54% on a NAV basis, compared to the S&P 500's total return of +15.20% over the same period — an outperformance of roughly 12.3 percentage points in that quarter, based on the two figures as reported.

**Volatility profile**

The source data does not include any quantitative volatility metrics. There is no standard deviation, beta, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, or other statistical risk measure provided. The only volatility-related content is a qualitative statement that the fund carries "characteristically high volatility and concentration risk associated with the Nasdaq-100." This is a general, non-numeric characterization and cannot substitute for a quantitative volatility figure. As a result, no assessment of the fund's actual historical volatility level, its trend over time, or its risk-adjusted return can be made from the data provided.

**Other explicitly noted data limitations**

- Tracking error versus the Nasdaq-100 Index was not found in the source data and is not available for this analysis.
- The YTD figures come from two different dates (August 4 and June 30, 2026) and two different return bases (dividend-reinvested vs. NAV), which the data does not reconcile.
- Whether the 3-, 5-, and 10-year return figures are annualized or cumulative is not specified in the source material.
- Holdings and sector weightings were reported inconsistently across sources and are not detailed in the performance data itself.
- All figures reflect a search snapshot as of August 13, 2026, and have not been cross-verified against Invesco's official fact sheet or SEC filings.

**Summary**

Within the bounds of the data supplied, QQQ shows strong absolute and relative (vs. S&P 500) returns across the periods reported, including apparent outperformance in Q2 2026. However, the return figures are drawn from inconsistent bases and dates, and the annualization status of the multi-year figures is unstated. Critically, no quantitative volatility data (standard deviation, beta, or similar) was provided — only a qualitative reference to high volatility and concentration risk — so the fund's volatility profile cannot be quantitatively characterized from this data set. This analysis does not constitute, and should not be read as, a recommendation to buy or sell.
