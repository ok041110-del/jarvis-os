## Fundamental Analysis — Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)
*Basis: Q4 FY2026 results, reported 2026-07-29 (data as compiled via web search, not verified against primary filings)*

**Revenue and Earnings Trend**

Microsoft's Q4 FY2026 revenue came in at $90.01B, up 18% year-over-year from $76.44B, and roughly 2.7% above the consensus estimate of $87.62B. Earnings outperformed by a wider margin: GAAP EPS of $4.81 and Non-GAAP EPS of $4.74 both beat the ~$4.24 consensus by roughly 12%, meaning profitability exceeded expectations by more than revenue did. That gap between the size of the revenue beat and the size of the EPS beat is suggestive of operating leverage or cost discipline, but the data provided does not include actual gross or operating margin figures, so this can only be treated as a directional signal, not a quantified margin outcome.

Growth was not uniform across the business. Intelligent Cloud revenue reached $39.3B (+32% YoY) and Productivity and Business Processes reached $37.8B (+14% YoY), meaning the cloud segment grew more than twice as fast as the productivity segment and outpaced the company-wide 18% growth rate. Within Intelligent Cloud, Azure revenue growth was reported at "+43% or more" YoY, and Azure's annualized run-rate crossed $100B for the first time. Microsoft Cloud revenue overall (the cross-segment cloud aggregate) was $59.3B, up 27% YoY — meaning cloud now represents about two-thirds of total quarterly revenue ($59.3B of $90.01B). The clear implication is a continuing mix shift toward cloud/Azure as the primary growth engine, with the non-cloud portions of the business growing more slowly by comparison.

**Margin Trend**

The provided data does not include explicit gross margin, operating margin, or segment-margin figures for this quarter or prior quarters, so no margin trend can be established from the data as given. The only available proxy is the earnings-versus-revenue beat differential described above (EPS beating consensus by a wider percentage than revenue did), which is consistent with — but does not confirm — margin expansion. Any statement beyond this would be inventing a number not present in the source data.

**Guidance**

The data includes only one forward-looking data point: on the earnings call, the CFO reaffirmed the company's calendar-year 2026 capital expenditure outlook. No specific revenue, earnings, or segment guidance figures for the next quarter or fiscal year were provided in the data. This is a meaningful gap — capex reaffirmation speaks to continued investment intensity (consistent with sustaining Azure/AI infrastructure buildout) but says nothing quantitative about expected future revenue or margin trajectory. Any assessment of forward growth expectations would be speculative given what's available here.

**Additional Context Provided**

Microsoft 365 Copilot was reported to have surpassed 30 million paid seats. The data does not include a comparable prior-period figure, so no trend (rate of adoption, acceleration/deceleration) can be derived from this single data point alone.

**What This Implies, and What It Doesn't**

Taken together, the data show a quarter of broad-based, cloud-led revenue growth with earnings outperformance exceeding revenue outperformance, and a market reaction (shares up as much as ~8% intraday post-earnings, per the data) that appears tied at least partly to the capex guidance reaffirmation rather than the earnings beat alone — implying investors read continued heavy investment as a signal of confidence in demand rather than as a margin concern, though this is an inference about market interpretation, not a fact stated outright in the data beyond the price move itself.

Two things should be stated explicitly as limitations rather than glossed over: (1) no margin percentages (gross, operating, or segment) are present in the source data, so this analysis cannot characterize a margin trend beyond the indirect EPS-beat proxy noted above; and (2) forward guidance data is limited to a single qualitative reference to capex plans, with no quantified revenue or earnings guidance provided — so statements about Microsoft's expected future performance cannot be supported from this data set. As instructed, this analysis does not offer an investment recommendation.
