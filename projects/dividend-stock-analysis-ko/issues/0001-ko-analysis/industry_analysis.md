## The Coca-Cola Company (KO) — Industry and Competitive Position Analysis

Based on the data provided, Coca-Cola occupies a position of clear scale leadership within the global non-alcoholic beverage industry, though several important caveats limit how precisely that leadership can be quantified.

**Scale and market capitalization.** As of June 10, 2026, Coca-Cola's market capitalization of approximately $360B stood well above that of its closest large-scale competitor, PepsiCo, at $211B — a gap of roughly $150B, or about 70% larger. Market capitalization is a financial-market valuation rather than a direct measure of operating share, but a gap of this magnitude is nonetheless consistent with the operating share data below and suggests investors are pricing Coca-Cola as the larger, more dominant franchise in its category.

**Global category position.** In the global non-alcoholic beverage market, Coca-Cola is reported to hold a share in the "40%-plus" range against PepsiCo's roughly 30%. This is a meaningfully wide margin, though the data does not specify the precise upper bound of Coca-Cola's share (i.e., whether it is 41% or closer to 49%), which limits precision in characterizing the exact size of the lead.

**U.S. carbonated soft drinks (CSD).** Within the U.S. CSD category specifically, Coca-Cola is reported at 47.1% share versus a PepsiCo figure of 16% for its U.S. "liquid beverage" segment. The data explicitly flags that these two figures are not directly comparable, since they appear to reference differently defined categories (CSD specifically vs. a broader liquid-beverage grouping for PepsiCo). This is a case where the underlying source material does not resolve the comparability question, so it would be inappropriate to characterize the ~31-point gap as a like-for-like measure of Coca-Cola's competitive advantage over PepsiCo in the U.S. market. At most, the CSD-only figure (47.1%) indicates a strong, near-plurality position within that narrower category.

**Regional variation.** Competitive position is not uniform across geographies. In India, the Coca-Cola portfolio (including Thumbs Up and Sprite) is reported at approximately 60% share versus PepsiCo's 30–35%, indicating a wide lead in that market. In developed European markets, Coca-Cola's CSD share is narrower, at 45–50%, suggesting the company's relative dominance varies by region and is generally most pronounced in markets like India and least pronounced (while still leading) in mature European markets. This regional spread is useful context: a single global or U.S. figure understates how competitive dynamics differ market by market.

**Industry growth trajectory.** The broader soft drink market is projected to grow from $283.8B in 2026 to $340.8B by 2031, a compound annual growth rate of 3.7%. This is a moderate, single-digit growth outlook rather than a high-growth category, consistent with a mature industry. The data identifies health-oriented reformulation, flavor innovation, and emerging categories such as prebiotic carbonated beverages as trends shaping category evolution — these represent areas where competitive share could shift going forward, though the provided data does not include company-specific figures on performance or investment in these sub-categories, so no conclusion can be drawn about which competitor is better positioned within them.

**Data limitations bearing on this analysis.** Several gaps constrain the confidence of the above picture:
- The CSD/liquid-beverage comparability issue noted above means the U.S. share gap between Coca-Cola and PepsiCo cannot be stated with the same confidence as the global or India figures.
- No time-series data is provided, so whether Coca-Cola's share (globally, in the U.S., in India, or in Europe) is stable, expanding, or contracting is not addressed by this dataset — the figures are a single point-in-time snapshot as of the search date (2026-08-14), drawn from secondary sources (accio.com, companieshistory.com, an SEC 8-K, thedishbloom.com) rather than primary financial filings reviewed in full.
- No data is provided on competitors beyond PepsiCo (e.g., Keurig Dr Pepper, private-label, or regional/local beverage players), so the "competitive landscape" described here is effectively a two-player comparison and may not reflect the full competitive set in any given market.
- No information was found regarding 2026 developments on sugar-tax policy, which is a potentially relevant regulatory factor for CSD category economics but is simply absent from the available data.
- The data does not quantify how tariff pressure or the $20B IRS tax dispute referenced elsewhere might affect competitive spending, pricing, or category investment, so no linkage can be drawn between those items and the market-share figures above.

**Summary characterization.** Within the limits of this data, Coca-Cola appears to hold a clear leadership position over PepsiCo by most of the reported share metrics — global non-alcoholic beverages, U.S. CSD (on a standalone basis), and both India and European CSD markets — with the degree of leadership varying meaningfully by geography (widest in India, narrowest in Europe). The industry itself is characterized as mature and moderate-growth (3.7% CAGR), with innovation in health-oriented and functional beverage sub-categories flagged as a trend rather than quantified. The most significant analytical caveat is the non-comparability of the U.S. category definitions used for Coca-Cola versus PepsiCo, which should not be read as a precise head-to-head figure without further clarification from primary sources.
