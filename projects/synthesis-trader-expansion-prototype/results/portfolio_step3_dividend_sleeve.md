**What it is:** an overlap/concentration judgment across the two dividend-sleeve positions, PG and JNJ, which neither Decision could have made.

**Why it's real:** PG and JNJ aren't just two unrelated HOLDs sitting side by side. Both Decisions independently flagged the *same category* of unresolved risk — a large, un-costed forward liability whose treatment in the numbers is ambiguous:

- PG: whether the ~$1B commodity/tariff/energy headwind is already absorbed into FY2027 guidance.
- JNJ: whether the $5.5B talc settlement (with a $3B tranche deferred to 2027) is already reserved for versus an incremental future charge.

Each Decision treated its own unresolved item as the single pivot for its own single-security stance, correctly, because that's the scope it was given. But at the portfolio level, these two "dividend-safety" positions (18% of the illustrative portfolio combined: 10% PG + 8% JNJ) are both sitting on unresolved downside catalysts that could land in the same window (FY2027) and both bear on the same underlying question the portfolio actually cares about — the reliability of the "dividend sleeve" as a defensive/income allocation. If both unresolved items resolve unfavorably around the same period, the portfolio's dividend-sleeve exposure moves together rather than independently, which is a correlation/concentration fact that only exists once you look at the two positions jointly. Neither Decision could see this because each was explicitly produced with no knowledge of the other security or of any portfolio context.

**What kind of judgment this is:** not a simple calculation (it's not just summing percentages) and not mechanical policy application (there's no stated max-position or max-sector policy to apply here, since none was configured). It is a genuinely new synthesis: recognizing that two nominally-independent HOLDs share a common unresolved risk driver and a common role in the portfolio (defensive dividend income), such that their individual "wait for disclosure" postures compound into a portfolio-level "this sleeve's defensiveness is more uncertain than either name alone suggests" fact. That synthesis did not and could not exist in either per-security Decision — it only emerges from reading both together against the portfolio state.

To be clear, this is a description of *what* needs judging, not a resolution of it — no position sizing or allocation call follows from this.
