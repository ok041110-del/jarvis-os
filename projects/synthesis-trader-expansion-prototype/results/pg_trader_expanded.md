## DECISION

- **Direction:** HOLD
- **Rationale:** The facts that are genuinely clean and uncontested — a guided second consecutive quarter of EPS pressure, a FY2027 guidance floor of flat Core EPS growth, an uncontested ~70% FCF-multiple premium to a key peer with no quantitative justification, and a technical setup below both major moving averages — are matched by equally uncontested positives: a 136-year dividend record, positive (if modest) top-line growth, a payout ratio still well below 100%, and a directionally unanimous analyst Buy lean. Neither case resolves the decisive gaps (FCF dividend coverage is entirely absent; whether the $1B headwind is already in guidance is undisclosed), so there isn't a clean factual basis to lean toward either BUY or SELL rather than sit with the uncertainty. This assessment covers only this single security's current-information directional stance — no portfolio context, existing position, or sizing information was provided or assumed.
- **Reassess when:** Whether the ~$1B commodity/tariff/energy headwind is already reflected in FY2027 guidance — resolving this would clarify whether the 0%-3% Core EPS range represents a fully-costed floor (supporting a more constructive stance) or an unabsorbed risk (supporting a more negative one), and it is the single disclosure most directly tied to the guidance figure both cases treat as pivotal.
