## STEP3-B (Risk assessment only, no composition recommendation)

No portfolio policy on position limits or look-through exposure caps was provided, so there is no defined threshold to judge whether a ~10.7–10.9% effective single-name weight is acceptable, excessive, or immaterial — the risk magnitude can be described, but its acceptability cannot be adjudicated without a stated limit. That absence is itself a gap: the portfolio has real correlated concentration in two event-driven names, and no stated rule exists to flag or bound it.
