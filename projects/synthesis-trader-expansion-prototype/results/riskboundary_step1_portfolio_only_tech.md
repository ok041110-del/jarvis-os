## PORTFOLIO COMPOSITION ASSESSMENT

**Composition: Hold all three positions unchanged (AAPL 10%, NVDA 10%, QQQ 10%).**

**Basis for this call:**

All three Trader Decisions independently returned HOLD, each stating explicitly that the underlying data does not support a confident directional call at this time. A HOLD on each name individually translates directly to "no trim, no add" at the portfolio level for that name — there is no per-security signal here pointing toward increasing or decreasing any position.

**One compositional fact worth noting, stated without a risk label:**

QQQ's disclosed composition shows AAPL at ~7.1–7.3% of QQQ's assets and NVDA at ~8.5–8.9% of QQQ's assets. This means the portfolio's stated 10% AAPL allocation and 10% NVDA allocation are not the full extent of exposure to those two names — each also exists inside the 10% QQQ holding. Restated as amounts: of the 10% allocated to QQQ, roughly 0.7–0.73 percentage points of portfolio value is attributable to AAPL and roughly 0.85–0.89 percentage points to NVDA, on top of the 10% direct holdings in each. This is a factual overlap in what underlies two different line items in the portfolio, not a judgment about whether that overlap is acceptable.

**Why this doesn't change the HOLD call:**

- No stated portfolio policy exists on position limits or look-through exposure caps, so there is no stated threshold this overlap can be measured against.
- The QQQ Decision separately notes that no risk-adjusted metrics were provided to weigh QQQ's concentration against its history, and that the decisive open variable (Fed path) is unresolved — so QQQ's own Decision doesn't produce a directional trim/add signal independent of the overlap fact.
- All three Decisions state their reassessment triggers are unresolved as of this review (Services trend-break vs. timing for AAPL, China licensing resolution for NVDA, Fed rate-path resolution for QQQ).

**Forward posture:** Hold the current composition. Because the reassessment triggers named in the AAPL and NVDA Decisions are the same facts that would also move QQQ's largest look-through components, watch those two triggers (AAPL Services trend clarity, NVDA China licensing resolution) alongside QQQ's own Fed-path trigger — any one resolving could be the basis to revisit AAPL, NVDA, and/or QQQ together, not just the single directly named security, given the look-through overlap documented above.
