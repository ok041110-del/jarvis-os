While this return figure is distinct from the distribution yield, it reinforces a broader pattern in the dataset: multiple metrics for EFA carry small but unexplained cross-source discrepancies, and the data does not provide enough detail to reconcile them.

This semiannual cadence means income arrives in fewer, likely larger, installments per year relative to quarterly-paying peers, though the data does not provide the actual per-payment dollar amounts or historical consistency of the semiannual schedule beyond naming the frequency.

Based on the data provided, there is no information suggesting EFA deviates from standard 1099 ETF tax reporting; however, the data also does not explicitly confirm 1099 treatment for EFA, so this should be read as "no unusual structure indicated" rather than a positive confirmation of standard treatment.

The yield discrepancy (3.36% vs. 2.75%) and the smaller average-return discrepancy (6.55% vs. 6.649%) remain unresolved in the source material.
