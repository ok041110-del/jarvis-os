Because this is a relative claim without a quantified basis (no basis-point figures, no time period, no methodology disclosed for how the comparison was derived), it should be treated as a directional signal rather than a measurable data point. **I want to flag explicitly: EFA's own tracking-error number is missing from this dataset**, so no independent assessment of how tightly EFA tracks the MSCI EAFE Index can be made from the data given.

**Construction Method as a Potential Source of Tracking Difference**

The data notes that EFA (like many broad international index funds) may use representative sampling rather than full replication of the underlying index. Representative sampling is a construction technique in which a fund holds a subset of index constituents chosen to approximate the index's risk/return characteristics, rather than holding every constituent in exact index weights. As a general matter, sampling-based replication can introduce tracking difference relative to full replication because the fund's actual holdings deviate somewhat from the index's precise composition. However, I want to be explicit that **the provided data does not state that this is a confirmed cause of EFA's tracking difference** — it only notes that sampling is used and that this "may" be a contributing factor. No causal link is asserted in the source material, and no data quantifies how much of any tracking difference (which itself is not numerically specified) would be attributable to this construction choice versus other factors (e.g., expense drag, foreign withholding tax treatment, currency effects, or fund cash flows — none of which are quantified in this dataset either).

- The underlying sources were not independently cross-checked against EFA's official fact sheet or the MSCI EAFE Index methodology document, and the data reflects a search snapshot from 2026-08-17 rather than real-time figures.

Based solely on the data provided: EFA has a stated 0.32% expense ratio, which is explicitly identified as higher than a same-benchmark competitor (VEA), and that same competitor is said to generally exhibit lower tracking error, though no tracking-error number for EFA exists in this dataset to substantiate the magnitude of that gap.
