Together, the top five countries account for over 60% of the portfolio. This is a materially concentrated geographic profile for a fund marketed as broad developed-markets-ex-North-America exposure: more than a fifth of the fund's weight sits in a single country (Japan), and the top two countries alone (Japan + UK) approach 40%.
