Procter & Gamble — Fundamental Analysis (based solely on the data provided)

**Revenue and earnings trend**

FY2026 (ended 2026-06-30) net sales were $87.0B, up 3% year-over-year, though organic sales growth was only +1%, indicating that reported growth was aided meaningfully by factors outside underlying volume/price/mix (e.g., currency or portfolio effects) — the data does not specify which. Full-year GAAP diluted EPS was $6.62 (+2%) and Core EPS was $6.89 (+1%), both showing modest full-year growth.

However, the most recent quarter (Q4 FY2026) tells a different story: net sales grew a slower +2%, while GAAP diluted EPS fell sharply, down 15% year-over-year to $1.26, and Core EPS declined 3% to $1.43. The data explicitly flags that it does not reconcile how a full year of +2% GAAP EPS growth coexists with a -15% GAAP EPS decline in the final quarter — meaning the trajectory implied by full-year results and the trajectory implied by the most recent quarter point in opposite directions, and the underlying driver of the Q4 GAAP decline (versus the smaller Core EPS decline) is not identified in the data provided.

**Margin trend**

No explicit gross margin or operating margin figures are provided in this data set. The only margin-relevant signal is the gap between GAAP EPS and Core EPS trends (Q4 GAAP -15% vs. Core EPS -3%), which suggests the GAAP decline was driven substantially by one-time or non-core items rather than by core operating performance — but since the nature of those items isn't specified, this remains an inference from the EPS gap rather than a directly reported margin figure.

**Guidance and its implications**

For FY2027, P&G guided to net sales growth of +1% to +3% (both all-in and organic), and Core EPS growth of 0% to 3% off the FY2026 Core EPS base of $6.89, implying a Core EPS range of roughly $6.89 to $7.11. The company also disclosed an expected headwind of approximately $1B (after-tax) in FY2027 from commodities, energy, and transportation costs, and stated that Q1 FY2027 EPS could decline more than 5% year-over-year.

Taken together, this guidance implies:
- Management expects only modest top-line growth (organic growth guidance of +1-3% is roughly in line with or somewhat above the +1% organic growth actually achieved in FY2026).
- Earnings growth is expected to be muted (0-3% Core EPS growth), consistent with the disclosed ~$1B input-cost headwind.
- The guidance explicitly anticipates a rough start to the fiscal year (Q1 EPS potentially down >5% YoY), suggesting management expects cost pressures to weigh most heavily early in FY2027, with the full-year range implying some improvement or offsetting actions later in the year — though the data does not specify what those offsetting actions would be.

**Explicit data limitations affecting this analysis**

Several inconsistencies in the source data limit the confidence of any margin or valuation-adjacent conclusions:

- No DCF or intrinsic value estimate is present in the data.

Given these gaps — particularly the unreconciled GAAP EPS direction (full year up, Q4 down sharply) and the unclear treatment of cost headwinds within guidance — this analysis is limited to describing what the data states rather than resolving these inconsistencies.
